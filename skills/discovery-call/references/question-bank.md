# Discovery question bank

Pick 6-10 per call. Never run the whole list. Questions are grouped by stage of the pain funnel and buying process.

## Opening and current state

- "What prompted you to take this call?"
- "Walk me through how you handle X today, start to finish."
- "Who's involved in that process day to day?"
- "What tools are part of it right now?"
- "If I shadowed your team for a day, what would I see?"

## Problems

- "What's working well that you'd want to keep?"
- "What's the most frustrating part?"
- "Where does it break when volume goes up?"
- "What do you find yourself doing manually that you wish you didn't?"
- "Can you give me a recent example where that hurt?"

## Impact

- "What does that cause downstream?"
- "Who else feels it? Which teams?"
- "How does that show up in your numbers?"
- "Does it affect customers directly?"
- "How does leadership see this problem?"

## Cost

- "Have you put a number on it?"
- "How many hours a week does that take, across the team?"
- "How many deals, tickets or customers does it affect a month?"
- "What would a 20% improvement be worth?"
- "If you had to defend fixing this to your CFO, what number would you use?"

## Personal stakes

- "How does this land on you personally?"
- "What are you measured on this year?"
- "If you fixed this, what would it mean for you and your team?"

## Urgency and why now

- "Why is this a priority now rather than last year?"
- "What happens if nothing changes in the next 6 months?"
- "Is there a deadline or event this is tied to? A launch, renewal, board meeting?"
- "Where does this rank against your other priorities this quarter?"

## Past attempts

- "What have you tried already?"
- "Why didn't it stick?"
- "If you've looked at other solutions, what did you like and not like?"
- "What would you do if you couldn't buy anything?"

## Decision process

- "Who else would weigh in on a decision like this?"
- "Who signs off in the end?"
- "Last time you bought something similar, how did it go step by step?"
- "Does anything need to go through security, legal or procurement?"
- "How long does that usually take here?"

## Budget

- "Is there budget set aside for this, or would it need a business case?"
- "Which budget would it come from?"
- "What's a typical investment range for a project like this at your company?"

## Success criteria

- "Six months after a project like this, how would you know it worked?"
- "Which metric would you show your boss?"
- "What would make you regret the decision?"

## Closing the call

- "Based on what you've heard, does it make sense to keep talking?"
- "What would you need to see in a next session?"
- "Who should be in that meeting?"
- "Can we put it on the calendar now?"
